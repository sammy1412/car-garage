Tool Summary — 7 Tools, All No-Code or Low-Code
🏪
GaragePlug
● No-Code · Free / $30 mo
Powers 7 steps: job card creation, parts inventory, job status portal, digital estimate, mechanic tablet app, e-invoice, and QR check-in. The central hub for everything.
📅
SimplyBook.me
● No-Code · Free / $10 mo
Powers 1 step: online booking page. Customer picks date and service, gets auto-confirmation. Share a link or embed on Facebook — no website needed.
💬
WhatsApp Business
● No-Code · Free
Powers 3 steps: booking confirmation, cost estimate approval (customer replies Yes/No), and photo sharing. Already used by most customers — zero learning curve.
⚡
Zapier / Make
◐ Low-Code · Free / $9 mo
Powers 1 step: auto-order parts. Drag-and-drop rule: "When GaragePlug stock is low → send reorder email to supplier." Takes 15 minutes to configure. Only Low-Code step in the whole process.
💳
SumUp / PayPal Business
● No-Code · ~1.7–2.9% per tx
Powers 1 step: digital payment. SumUp card reader (EUR 39 one-time) for tap-to-pay at counter, or send PayPal link by SMS for online payment. No monthly fee.
🔌
OBD-II Scanner + Car Scanner App
▲ Hardware · EUR 30–80 one-time
Powers 1 step: car inspection. Bluetooth dongle plugs into every car's dashboard port. Free app shows fault codes in plain English. Mechanic copies into GaragePlug.
📊
Google Looker Studio
● No-Code · Free
Powers 1 step: analytics dashboard. Export GaragePlug data as CSV → Google Sheet → drag-and-drop charts. Shows revenue, mechanic performance, parts usage trends. Completely free.
