Level 3 — Component Diagram

What are the components inside GaragePlug (the core system)?

Zooms inside the GaragePlug GMS container and shows each functional module, what it does in the process, and which BPMN step it powers.

GaragePlug GMS — Internal Components
🔲
Component
QR Check-in Module
Generates unique QR codes per booking. When scanned at drop-off, logs arrival time and triggers job card creation
BPMN Step: Drop Off Car
🗃️
Component
Job Card Engine
Creates and manages digital job records. Integrates VIN lookup to auto-fill vehicle details. Links customer, vehicle, parts, and mechanic
BPMN Step: Create Digital Job Card
📦
Component
Inventory Manager
Tracks real-time parts stock. Checks availability per job. Sends low-stock alert to Zapier for auto-reorder. Auto-deducts when parts are used
BPMN Step: Auto Parts Check
💰
Component
Estimate Generator
Calculates total cost from parts prices + labour rate × time. Generates itemised PDF estimate. Sends shareable link to customer by SMS/WhatsApp
BPMN Step: Generate Digital Estimate
📲
Component
Mechanic Tablet App
Mobile interface for mechanics. Displays assigned jobs, parts list, digital checklist. Captures photos. Marks job complete to trigger invoice
BPMN Steps: Receive Job Card, Log Work, Photo Upload
📄
Component
Invoice Engine
Auto-generates PDF invoice when job is marked complete. Applies approved estimate or calculates from job record. Sends push notification to customer
BPMN Step: Generate E-Invoice
🔔
Component
Notification Service
Sends automated SMS messages at key events: booking confirmed, car received, estimate ready, car ready, invoice sent. Integrates with WhatsApp Business
BPMN Steps: All notification steps
📊
Component
Reporting Module
Built-in dashboard for revenue, job count, mechanic utilisation. Exports data as CSV for Google Looker Studio for deeper custom analytics
BPMN Step: Update Analytics Dashboard
👥
Component
Customer Database
Stores customer profiles, contact details, vehicle history, past service records, and communication logs. Linked to job cards and invoices
Supports: All customer-facing steps
🚗
Component
Vehicle Registry
Stores vehicle records linked by VIN. Integrates with external VIN decoder to auto-populate make, model, year, engine type at job card creation
Supports: Create Job Card step
✅
Component
Approval Tracker
Records customer approval or rejection of cost estimates with timestamp. Creates digital audit trail. Links back to job card for compliance
BPMN Step: Approve Cost Estimate
🔗
Component
Integration API
Connects GaragePlug to external tools: Zapier (stock alerts), SimplyBook.me (bookings), payment processors (SumUp/PayPal), and Looker Studio (data export)
Supports: All cross-system flows
Component (functional module inside GaragePlug)
