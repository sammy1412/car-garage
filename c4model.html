Who uses the system and what does it connect to?

The big picture. Shows the three types of users, the garage digital system as a whole, and every external service it depends on. Databases are not shown at this level — they appear in C2 and C3.

Level 2 — Container Diagram with 3-Layer Storage

Applications, databases, and how data flows between them

Shows each deployable application and all three storage layers. Databases are drawn as cylinders. Arrows show direction of data flow.

3-Layer Storage Architecture
① Operational Layer
🗄️ GaragePlug Cloud DB
Short-term daily transaction data. Job cards, parts usage, work logs, invoices. Managed by GaragePlug vendor.
② Customer Data Layer
🗄️ HubSpot CRM Database
Long-term permanent customer records. Single source of truth for all customer profiles, vehicle history, and service records. Synced from GaragePlug via Zapier.
③ Analytics Layer
🗄️ Google BigQuery
Historical aggregated data for dashboards. Receives exports from GaragePlug and HubSpot via Zapier. Feeds Looker Studio for KPI reporting. Free tier.
