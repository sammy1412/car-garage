Tool Summary — 7 Tools, All No-Code or Low-Code
🏪
GaragePlug
● No-Code · Free / $30 mo
Powers 7 steps: job card, parts inventory, job status, estimate, mechanic tablet app, e-invoice, QR check-in. The central hub.
📅
SimplyBook.me
● No-Code · Free / $10 mo
Powers 1 step: online booking page. Auto-sends confirmation. Share a link or embed on Facebook.
💬
WhatsApp Business
● No-Code · Free
Powers 3 steps: confirmation, estimate approval (Yes/No reply), and photo sharing with customer.
⚡
Zapier / Make
◐ Low-Code · Free / $9 mo
Powers 1 step: auto-order parts. Drag-and-drop rule in Zapier. Only Low-Code step in the whole process.
💳
SumUp / PayPal Business
● No-Code · ~1.7–2.9% per tx
Powers 1 step: digital payment. Card reader (EUR 39) or online payment link. No monthly fee.
🔌
OBD-II Scanner + Car Scanner App
▲ Hardware · EUR 30–80 one-time
Powers 1 step: car inspection. Bluetooth dongle + free app shows all fault codes in plain English.
📊
Google Looker Studio
● No-Code · Free
Powers 1 step: analytics dashboard. Connect Google Sheet from GaragePlug export → drag-and-drop charts. 100% free.
