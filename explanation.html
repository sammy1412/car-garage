Car Garage · Digital Transformation · System Explanation

How Our Digital Garage
System Works

A plain-English guide to the technology behind your car service — from the moment you book to the moment you pay.

Built for customers · No technical knowledge needed

Our garage has replaced all paper forms, phone calls, and manual filing with a connected digital system. Every step of your car service — booking, inspection, cost approval, repair, and payment — is now handled automatically through software. This page explains what that means for you in simple, everyday language.

01 — The People

Three people use this system

The system is designed around three roles. Each person interacts with a different part of the technology.

👤
Role 1

You — The Customer

You book online, receive automatic updates by SMS or WhatsApp, approve any extra costs from your phone, and pay digitally when you pick up your car. You never need to call the garage or wait on hold.

App · WhatsApp · SMS
🔧
Role 2

The Mechanic

Receives your job details on a tablet — no paper. Scans your car's diagnostic port to read fault codes automatically. Logs all work done digitally, takes before-and-after photos, and marks the job complete to trigger your invoice.

Tablet App · OBD-II Scanner
👔
Role 3

The Garage Manager

Monitors all jobs live on a dashboard. Sees which parts are running low, how much revenue has been made, and how each mechanic is performing — all updated in real time without any manual counting or reporting.

Dashboard · CRM · Analytics
02 — Your Journey

What happens at each step of your service

From the moment you book to the moment you drive away — here is exactly what the system does, and what you experience.

1
You book online

Pick a date and service — any time, day or night

Visit the booking page on your phone or computer. Choose your service type and an available slot. The system automatically sends you a confirmation by SMS or email — no phone call needed, no waiting for someone to pick up. Tool: SimplyBook.me

2
You drop off your car

Scan the QR code at the entrance — done in 10 seconds

When you arrive, scan the QR code posted at the garage entrance with your phone camera. This logs your drop-off time automatically and creates your digital job record — no paper form to fill in. You immediately receive a link to track your car's progress live. Tool: GaragePlug

3
The garage checks your car

Your car is scanned and inspected — results logged automatically

The mechanic plugs a small Bluetooth device into your car's diagnostic port (under the dashboard — every car has one). Within 30 seconds, all fault codes are read and stored in your digital job record. Your vehicle history from previous visits is already there too — no need to explain it again. Tool: OBD-II Scanner + GaragePlug

4
You approve the cost (if needed)

Get the estimate on your phone — approve with one tap

If any additional work is needed beyond your original booking, the system automatically calculates the cost and sends you a detailed estimate via WhatsApp or SMS. You approve or decline with a single tap — no phone call, no back-and-forth. Your decision is recorded with a timestamp as your digital agreement. Tool: GaragePlug + WhatsApp

5
You collect and pay

Receive a push notification — pay online or tap-to-pay

The moment the mechanic marks your job as complete, the system auto-generates your invoice and sends you a push notification: "Your car is ready." You can pay via the payment link in the message before you arrive, or tap-to-pay with your card at the counter. A digital receipt is sent automatically — no paper. Tool: GaragePlug + SumUp / PayPal

04 — Your Data

Where your information is stored — and why it is safe

Your data is never stored in a spreadsheet or a filing cabinet. It lives in three separate, professional cloud databases — each with a specific purpose.

①

Daily Operations Database

Stores your current job in real time — every part used, every step completed, your invoice, your approval. Managed securely by GaragePlug in the cloud. Updated live as your car is being serviced.

🏪 GaragePlug
Cloud DB
②

Customer Records Database

Stores your long-term profile permanently — your contact details, all your vehicles (with their service history), and every visit you have made to the garage. This means next time you visit, your mechanic already knows your car. Managed securely by HubSpot CRM.

👥 HubSpot CRM
Database
③

Analytics Database

Stores aggregated historical data — revenue trends, most common repairs, parts usage patterns. Used only for management reporting through Google Looker Studio dashboards. No personal customer data is used in this layer — only anonymised totals and averages.

📊 Google BigQuery
+ Looker Studio
